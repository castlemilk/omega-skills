All scenes below are original prompt proposals, not descriptions of additional frames or claims of successful generation. Replace a recipe's subject and constraints with the user's brief. The bundled construction atlas supports the visual vocabulary; it does not require every future image to depict spacecraft.

For open worldbuilding, apply [monumental-direction.md](monumental-direction.md) and run the creative-writing loop before choosing a recipe. These recipes solve composition and detail; they must not replace invention or restrict new civilizations to industrial metal, human habitats or six familiar palettes. Use the selected world's construction logic when it calls for living, fluid, optical, geological or field-based forms. Keep monumental presence, scale and material depth explicit in the prompt; never rely on the fictional title alone to carry the theme. Product and website adaptations are for those requested modes.

## Distilled master prompt

> Create an original [format and finish] monumental science-fiction artwork showing [concrete subject and one event or organizing relationship]. In this world, [one premise with a visible consequence]. Arrange [immense forms, distributed system or environmental boundary] from [viewpoint], with [compatible scale relationships, depth and continuation beyond the frame]. Make [two concrete consequences] visible. Use [substantial materials or environmental structure] appropriate to this world, with detail concentrated at [meaningful locations]. Light from [source], using [palette roles and atmosphere]. Preserve [user constraints] and leave [intentional mystery] unexplained. Omit unrelated text or interface elements.

Use the world-first bridge below to develop this template. Replace the default theme and scale when a precise user brief calls for another style or an intimate object. Use the industrial template only when its machinery and finish fit the concept.

## Industrial reference-style template

> Create an original retrofuturist illustration of [subject]. Build the image around [one dominant silhouette] and [one clear scale relationship], with broad quiet space around the focal form. Use [dominant palette], [supporting material color], and a restrained [accent]. Give the structure a smooth readable outer mass, then concentrate intricate ribs, service panels, conduits, and small fittings around [specific location]. Render tactile painted surfaces, fine technical linework, selective worn edges, and atmospheric depth. Light the scene from [source]. Keep the geometry coherent, the focal point clear, and the background simpler. No captions or unrelated interface graphics.

## Expanded prompt template

```text
Create an original [format / intended-use] illustration of [subject and action].

The visual idea is [one spatial or scale relationship]. Compose it from [viewpoint], with [main form] in [placement], [supporting form] behind it, and [quiet region] left open. Use [small recognizable element or repeated module] to establish scale. Keep [subject-specific invariant] intact.

Organize the image through [primary silhouette / distributed system / boundary]. Develop it with [supporting rhythm or interaction]. Concentrate fine detail at [locations]: [component + connection or environmental relationship], [placement], and [material behavior]. Keep [named broad surfaces or quiet regions] calm when appropriate.

Use [material 1] with [surface response], [material 2] at the joins, and [material 3 only if needed]. Suggest [maintenance / age] through [localized wear]. Render [painted / graphic / photographic finish] with [edge and mark treatment].

Use [dominant field], [supporting color], and [accent at a named focal element]. Light from [source and direction], with [shadow behavior] and [atmospheric separation]. Preserve the large value masses at thumbnail size.

Avoid [three or four relevant failure modes]. [Exact text or empty-space requirement only if needed.]
```

## World-first prompt bridge

```text
Invented premise: [capability] lets [inhabitants] choose [practice or purpose],
OR [condition] produces [emergent process and visible consequence].
The selected visual direction is [monumental science fiction, unless overridden].
The image catches [concrete sci-fi subject and one event or spatial relationship].
Make the premise visible through [consequence A], [consequence B], and optionally [C].
Build the organizing form, distributed system or boundary and its material behavior from those consequences.
Choose [viewpoint, crop, depth and compatible scale evidence] to make that relationship readable.
Make the world feel larger than the frame through [occlusion, distances or continued structure].
Use [palette, motivated light, atmosphere and finish] appropriate to this world.
Keep [user invariants]; omit lore that has no visible role.
```

Keep the full world card separate. Do not paste a civilization's entire history into the image prompt. For a new-world set, compare purpose, mechanism and silhouette before rendering; for an established-world set, preserve canon and vary events and viewpoints.

## Organic-techno fusion prompt

Use when the brief asks for biological and technological convergence. Develop the shared function before selecting materials; retain the monumental direction unless the user requests another scale.

> Create an original monumental science-fiction artwork of [ship, structure, monolith or planetary work] during [one event]. In this world, [biological process] and [technological role] have become [one shared system]. Make that convergence visible at [specific location]: [material or structural organization] continues through [junction or specialized region] into [technological form], following [load, transport, sensing or growth relationship]. Show [a second visible consequence]. Compose from [viewpoint] with [nested scale and depth evidence]. Use [distinct materials, palette and motivated light], preserving broad quiet masses and precise local detail. Keep [user invariants]. The living and manufactured qualities should belong to the same construction.

For mature seamless convergence, replace an explicit interface with a specialized region or visible behavior. Use original severe, mineral, membrane, crystalline or geological forms as readily as soft ones. Avoid decorative vegetation on an otherwise unchanged machine, arbitrary flesh-metal halves, and repeating one shell-like silhouette. Keep unpictured capabilities in the scene card.

## 1. Monumental aperture

**Use for:** quiet grandeur, alien civil engineering, portrait posters.

> Create a tall retrofuturist illustration of a remote observation structure inside an immense pale industrial enclosure. A slender stepped tower rises from the lower third, while an enormous circular opening sits high behind it, offset rather than perfectly centered. Make the tower small enough for the surrounding wall to feel overwhelming. A few tiny service doors establish scale. Preserve a broad unbroken wall plane and a deep quiet shadow below the tower.
>
> Shape the opening from three nested collars and a receding sequence of radial ribs. Concentrate fine mechanical detail on the tower's lower platforms: recessed hatches, railings, cable trays, small support brackets, and layered metal lips. Let these details follow one consistent construction rhythm. Keep the outer collar smooth except for fine seams and subtle wear at its exposed edge.
>
> Use chalk white, graphite, and muted steel green with a few small amber windows. Pale side light reveals the surface relief; the inner throat fades into cool darkness. Render it as a tactile painted science-fiction illustration with fine technical linework, broken brush marks, and restrained grain. Keep the silhouette and depth legible before the microdetail. No captions, decorative HUD, or global neon outlines.

**Change one variable:** replace the tower with a suspended service bridge; retain the circle and quiet wall. For a broader variation, invert the scale relationship while keeping the material language.

## 2. Warm industrial harbor

**Use for:** intricate machinery, inhabited engineering, energetic angled compositions.

> Create an original portrait illustration looking obliquely down into a suspended orbital maintenance harbor. A broad ochre slab crosses the upper frame; beneath its calm painted surface, a dense but ordered service spine opens toward a dark rectangular bay. One small cream utility craft approaches the entry, providing scale and a clear narrative focus. A distant violet atmosphere keeps the lower background quiet.
>
> Build the harbor from stepped frames, inset docking recesses, and long service channels. Place the smallest details at the bay mouth and underside: parallel conduits secured by spaced clamps, recessed grilles, ladder runs, paired fasteners, and thin rails that connect usable platforms. Show subtle paint wear at exposed corners and darker stains below selected joints. Keep the broad slab faces simple.
>
> Use ochre and oxidized orange as structural colors, pale cream on the craft, and cool blue-violet in the deepest shadows. Warm grazing light catches the upper rims; only a small entry beacon emits light. Render fine etched-looking marks within painterly masses, with selective hard edges and distant detail dissolved by haze. Avoid random greeble noise, all-over rust, floating labels, and unrelated glowing strips.

## 3. Lucid pastel station

**Use for:** calm futures, graceful megastructures, a less militarized direction.

> Create a portrait retrofuturist artwork of a slender shell-white sail station suspended above a still turquoise expanse. Give it one long curved plane rising from a compact root and a low horizontal deck that nearly touches its dark reflection. Frame it with a small cropped architectural edge in the foreground, leaving much of the sky open. A tiny maintenance pod near the root establishes scale without becoming the main subject.
>
> Keep the sail face broad and almost uninterrupted. Let its seams follow the curve. Place restrained intricacy only at the root and underside: nested terraces, thin railings, recessed access panels, and a short band of warm windows. Use shell white, dusty cyan, muted coral, and a small amber accent; treat these as color roles, allowing soft transitions.
>
> Use gentle daylight, a cool reflected fill, and a soft atmospheric horizon. Render the surface as a painted illustration with visible but quiet brushwork and precise focal edges. Preserve its elegance and clear negative space. Avoid dense city clutter, aggressive weaponry, heavy grime, and luminous outlines around the entire structure.

## 4. Cosmic solitude

**Use for:** wonder, distance, narrative stillness, cover artwork.

> Create a contemplative science-fiction illustration of a single traveler on a narrow mineral terrace looking toward a vast banded celestial sphere. Place the traveler low in the frame and the sphere partly beyond the top edge. Separate foreground rock, middle-distance cloud, and the distant sphere with progressively softer contrast. Let the empty atmosphere occupy more space than the traveler and the ledge.
>
> Keep the silhouette of the terrace unusual but readable: one long overhang with a few tapering mineral projections beneath it. Place a small weathered observation device near the traveler, with a quiet aperture, a hinged housing, and two short cable connections. Make the rock tactile through broad planes and directional marks, not uniform photographic noise.
>
> Use dusty peach, muted teal, and violet shadow. Let the sphere carry a restrained banded color rhythm while pale skylight defines the ledge. Render painterly atmospheric transitions and precise detail only around the small observation point. No extra crowd, city skyline, caption, or bright light effects competing with the scale relationship.

**Adaptation:** when a brief excludes people, use the device and a small access path as scale cues. Do not add a traveler by habit.

## 5. Emissive infrastructure

**Use for:** high contrast, deep industrial space, controlled cyan or magenta accents.

> Create a vertically framed illustration looking down through a sequence of immense dark rectangular frames into a luminous cyan reservoir far below. Offset the viewpoint so that the nearest frame cuts diagonally across the top edge. Build depth through three clear levels rather than an endless uniform tunnel. Keep the nearest structure almost black and reserve a quiet dark region along one side.
>
> Detail the near and middle frames with layered plate edges, inset service channels, railings, and small platforms connected by visible supports. Align conduits with the architecture and terminate them at recessed couplings. Let far structures simplify into thin bands within the haze. The reservoir illuminates only surfaces facing it; a few tiny warm maintenance lights provide scale.
>
> Use blue-black structure, oxidized teal surfaces, pale cyan light, and sparse amber points. Render a tactile painted technical surface with narrow linework and occasional rough marks. Keep the luminous area shaped and contained. Avoid an all-over glow, floating interface elements, identical high-contrast detail at every depth, and excessive bloom that erases the architecture.

## 6. Biomechanical relic

**Use for:** ambiguous alien artifacts, organic engineering, intricate curved detail.

> Create a quiet futuristic illustration of a huge asymmetric shell-like instrument suspended just above a pale mineral plain. Its folded oval aperture faces slightly away from the viewer, revealing a darker interior rather than a luminous portal. A small survey cart beside the shadow provides scale. Keep the surrounding landscape sparse and the horizon low.
>
> Organize the shell with thick ribs that converge near the lower root and finer folds that follow their direction. Route two flexible conduits into recessed attachment points. Add layered rim segments, small clamped joints, and patches of mineral discoloration along protected ridges. Keep the large outer contour continuous and the interior readable. Suggest grown structure meeting maintained machinery without turning the whole surface into tangled cables.
>
> Use muted patinated teal, mineral olive, warm sand, and a tiny cream indicator on the cart. Soft high light defines the folds; a restrained reflected glow rises from the plain. Render painterly texture, selective fine contours, and softened distant edges. Avoid an identifiable face, arbitrary appendages, bright rainbow bioluminescence, and captions.

## 7. Clean future instrument

**Use for:** an explicitly requested product, desk object, or restrained brand asset. Do not select this recipe for an open monumental world exploration.

> Create a square illustration of a compact speculative environmental instrument resting on a pale surface. Give it a memorable smooth wedge silhouette, a dark recessed sensing aperture, and a slightly lifted rear edge. Use an intimate three-quarter view and a grounded contact shadow. Keep ample clear space around the object; do not add buildings, spacecraft, or people.
>
> Render a warm white ceramic-like outer shell with a dark metal insert. Concentrate the intricate detail in a single exposed service recess: one flush cover, a continuous gasket seam, a precise vent rhythm, and a few paired fasteners. Add a small amber status indicator and subtle fine machining marks on the insert. Keep the broad shell clean and calm.
>
> Use soft directional daylight and restrained painted technical linework. The result should feel precise, tangible, and quietly futuristic, with detail visible on inspection rather than at every edge. No all-over grime, glowing perimeter, branding, or invented interface text.

**Boundary:** this is concept artwork, not a validated product design or manufacturing drawing. Do not invent physical feasibility claims.

## 8. Cuttlefish-shaped exploration vessel

**Use for:** adapting the references to a new organic silhouette without copying a source scene.

> Create an original retrofuturist illustration of a cuttlefish-shaped exploration vessel drifting through a vast blue-green atmosphere. Preserve a rounded mantle, a continuous rippling fin edge, and a compact cluster of forward appendage-like sensor booms. Give the vessel one strong readable silhouette and place it across the middle of a wide composition, with a soft pale cloud bank behind it for scale. Keep the background spacious.
>
> Translate the mantle into broad overlapping shell panels with fine curved seams. Concentrate the machinery along the ventral service spine and the roots of the sensor booms: small coupling collars, flexible conduit loops, recessed lenses, and a few narrow radiator fins. Let flexible parts follow the implied motion. Keep the upper mantle smooth, with subtle painterly banding and a few warm mineral-colored panels.
>
> Use muted teal, warm ivory, dark graphite, and small amber indicators. Soft side light defines the body; low-contrast atmosphere separates it from the distance. Render tactile painted surfaces and selective technical linework. No jets attached at random, extra fins, mechanical clutter that erases the animal-derived form, or captions.

**Boundary:** use a real animal's anatomy when the user asks for an animal. Do not automatically convert it into a machine. For a living cuttlefish, transfer palette, scale, edge treatment, and fine skin patterning rather than metal panels.

## 9. Website hero

**Use for:** wide editorial artwork that leaves usable room for interface text.

> Create a wide futuristic editorial illustration for a website hero. Place a monumental curved structure in the right half, with an immense dark opening and a small lit service platform near its base. Keep the left third a low-contrast atmospheric field with no focal detail so that real website typography can be overlaid later. Do not render the heading into the image.
>
> Use broad graphite architecture, warm ivory planes, and restrained oxidized copper detail. Concentrate fine ribs, service panels, rails, and small fittings around the opening and the platform. Keep the outer mass calm. Pale side light catches selected edges, with a small amber glow at the platform and soft blue-gray distance. Render painterly technical surfaces and a clear large-scale silhouette. Avoid floating dashboards, random UI markings, text, and excessive light effects. Keep the focal structure inside a crop-safe region for narrower screens.

**Adaptation:** if brand rules require an obsidian background, preserve separation through value and material response rather than filling the entire object with neon. Inspect the eventual page crop separately; the illustration alone does not prove responsive behavior or text contrast.

## 10. Consistent series

For a connected-world series, use selected shared traits, then write a separate composition for each image. For an exploratory series of unrelated worlds, share only the requested finish and use different causes, material systems, palettes and environments. Do not apply the sample palette below universally.

```text
Shared visual language:
Chalky warm-white outer planes; muted teal service structures; amber only at focal access points; fine graphite-like technical linework; painterly atmosphere; clean distant silhouettes; intricacy concentrated at joints and undersides.

Image A — threshold:
An offset circular aperture above a small service bridge; portrait; quiet dark lower half.

Image B — voyage:
A smooth swept vessel crossing a pale sky; wide; service detail confined to its lower spine.

Image C — dwelling:
Stacked rounded habitats with deep cutaway undersides; daylight; one small transport route as scale cue.

Image D — discovery:
A folded organic instrument above a mineral plain; low horizon; patinated folds around a dark aperture.
```

Keep palette roles, mark quality, material treatment, and recurring detail motifs stable. Vary geometry, viewpoint, and narrative role. Do not merely recolor the same arrangement four times. Use actual reference outputs for later edits when supported; do not promise deterministic continuity from these notes alone.

## Precision edit prompts

**More intricate, same composition**

> Preserve the existing subject, silhouette, palette, lighting, viewpoint, and background. Add fine structure only inside the underside service bay: connected cable runs, paired clamps, recessed access panels, thin louvres, and a few small couplings. Keep the broad outer planes and empty areas unchanged. Make all new lines follow the existing perspective and curvature.

**Less generic science fiction**

> Preserve the subject and composition. Replace arbitrary glowing strips with one clearly located light source. Simplify unrelated surface ornaments into a coherent set of ribs, panels, and connected service components. Give the large shape a clear silhouette and vary detail density between focal and distant areas.

**More painterly**

> Keep the geometry, colors, focal position, and light direction. Introduce selective broken brush marks on broad surfaces, fine technical contours at joins, and soft scumbled atmosphere in the distance. Reduce uniform photographic sharpness. Preserve the readability of focal details.

**Quieter future**

> Preserve the object and its key details. Lower background contrast, widen the calm atmospheric area, soften distant structure, and retain only a few localized lights. Keep the focal silhouette crisp and the palette restrained.

**Clean maintained version**

> Preserve the geometry and material divisions. Remove generalized rust, scratches, and dirt from broad faces. Keep small realistic wear only at access edges and contact points. Retain precision seams, gaskets, and the existing status light.

## Motion extension

When supplied footage is an artwork montage, distinguish cuts and whole-image reframing from independent scene motion. Do not infer continuous modeled motion or a production technique from image panning.

For a new animation brief, define separately:

- **Camera:** for example a slow lateral drift with a small push inward.
- **Subject:** for example a gradual vessel turn or a restrained wave along a fin edge.
- **Environment:** for example a low-contrast cloud drift behind fixed architecture.
- **Secondary detail:** for example one slow indicator pulse or a subtle change in aperture light.
- **Loop constraint:** align pose, lighting, background, and velocity at the start and end when a seamless loop is requested.

Keep mechanical details attached to the body and consistent between frames. Avoid synchronized motion of every component. Use the actual video, animation, or code tools requested; deliver a storyboard or keyframe as such when no motion has been rendered.
