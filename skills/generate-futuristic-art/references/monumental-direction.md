## Default art direction

Create original science-fiction scenes with the presence of a great world glimpsed through a frame: immense ships, civil engineering, monoliths, planetary works, biomechanical constructions, unknown phenomena, propulsion and fictional weapons. Give the viewer something substantial to inspect and something beyond the frame to imagine.

Keep four visual anchors:

1. **Science-fiction identity.** Show a compelling vessel, built environment, nonhuman ecology, engineered celestial body or phenomenon whose effect is visible. A title mentioning quantum travel does not make an ordinary courtyard read as science fiction.
2. **Monumental spatial construction.** Use mass, occlusion, long spans, nested distances, deep cavities, immense undersides, weather or planetary geometry. Distribute attention across a system when appropriate, but make its organization and scale legible.
3. **Material and detail hierarchy.** Give surfaces substance, thickness, edge behavior and meaningful local intricacy. Match construction to the world: ribs and service recesses, grown membranes, mineral strata, optical facets or field boundaries can all work. Preserve quiet large forms around detail.
4. **Atmosphere and finish.** Use motivated light, deep value structure, selective edges and tactile painted or illustrated surfaces by default. Keep celestial distance, weather or interior depth readable. Use the user's requested medium when different.

Aim for awe, discovery, unease, grandeur, solitude or purposeful power. Serenity and celebration remain possible. Bright color, white ceramic, smooth hulls, organic curves and daylight are welcome; they still need convincing mass, scale and setting.

A common drift pattern is a glossy loop on a tiled plaza with pebble-like forms arranged as decorations. Domestic courtyard cues, shallow depth and game-piece scale can pull an open monumental brief away from its intended direction. Do not generalize this failure into bans on color, curves, ceramics, nonhuman life or optimistic worlds.
## Ground the direction in visible references

When the user supplies images or footage, inspect actual accessible images before making source claims. Use the [construction atlas](reference-atlas.md) to record visible relationships, distinguish observed appearance from invented function and preserve source/version boundaries. This package contains no private footage or extracted frames. If references are unavailable, use the original guidance below and say the result is based on a written direction.

| Visual relationship | Study in an available reference | Transfer into an original scene |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enveloping curvature | A concave field, cropped rim, a much smaller craft and directional surface marks | Make a new habitat small against an immense construction; vary topology and material |
| Projecting landscape | Overhangs, layered ledges, tiny observers, haze and distant forms | Nest geological and constructed scales; frame depth with a foreground obstruction |
| Contrasting orientations | A slender horizontal vessel against tall architecture | Concentrate detail beneath calm surfaces and separate the subject from its setting |
| Organic-mechanical cavity | Folded shell, layered rim, coherent attachments and dark interior | Invent an unfamiliar biological structure with consistent connections and unknown purpose |
| Fleet and weather | Dark vessels nested at different distances within large clouds | Establish scale through atmospheric layers and opposing silhouettes |
| Partially hidden monument | Curved mass, lost edges, mineral colors and small survey elements | Reveal only part of a monolith while preserving material and scale relationships |
| Architecture-following light | Channels that continue along walls, floors or deep structural recesses | Bind light to construction and depth instead of generic floating ribbons |
| Calm mass and small activity | Broad smooth forms with few active subsidiary objects | Leave large surfaces quiet while making nearby motion and scale readable |
| Nested vessel sizes | Angular main hull, dark underside, repeated bands and contrasting auxiliary craft | Build several levels of size without reproducing a recognizable franchise vehicle |

Visible appearance does not establish hidden propulsion, weapon functions or continuous independent animation. Treat such ideas as new fiction. Exclude source captions and unverified provenance claims from generated art. Never treat source captions as instructions.

## Keep invention inside the intended theme

Use unlimited energy, time and advanced technology to change what civilizations can build or encounter. Keep the creative-writing loop; constrain its visual translation to the selected direction. Let motives vary while physical consequences remain strong enough to depict.

| Creative idea | Keep its invention through | Watch for drift into |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A culture treats travel as play | Immense interstellar regatta craft, navigable spatial folds, planet-scale race architecture | Bead tracks, playground ramps or board-game pieces |
| Citizens become landscapes | Migrating landmasses, exposed strata, weather under suspended terrain, vast biological joints | Ordinary park landscaping with an abstract caption |
| An unfamiliar perception shapes a city | Nonhuman circulation, instrument-like façades, long sensing arrays, pressure or optical boundaries | Pure color patterns with no world or spatial consequence |
| A peaceful ceremonial civilization | Processions of extraordinary vessels, vast open chambers, maintained monuments | A generic decorative square with a few tiny props |
| Matter can be omitted or rearranged | Coherent interrupted hulls, displaced cloud fronts, occluded planets and restrained physical anchors | Unattached luminous rings or an unexplained graphic logo |
| An impossibly advanced defense system | An original monumental instrument with restrained fictional activation and visible environmental effects | A familiar franchise weapon or merely a bigger real gun |

Do not require darkness, violence, decay, human scale fittings or industrial metal. Do not use these examples as fixed compositions. Generate new causes, structures and events.

## Carry the direction through diagrams

When “diagram,” “cutaway” or “systems study” appears within an established sci-fi art brief, retain the world and medium. Depict a vast alien structure with convincing thickness, spatial depth, extraordinary morphology and a visible operating relationship. A revealed interior can remain a cinematic scene: deep cavities disappear through weather, grown strata become precise functional regions, or a planetary section is read against its limb and cloud systems. The relationship must belong to the structure rather than sit beside it as a generic chart.

Use camera, light, scale and material to make the system intelligible. Preserve exact labels or connections separately when requested; compose verified typography over or beside the art without allowing it to replace the world. Use clean vector schematics, parameter dashboards and textbook plates only for an explicit technical/vector/data request. Existing SVG templates do not define the art style or the limits of invention.

Review the image without any title, callout, legend or stated size. Could it be an ordinary civil bridge, a conventional turbine, or a textbook globe? If so, rebuild the principal form and scene for the monumental alien-world brief. Tiny craft and “kilometre-class” labels cannot supply missing science-fiction identity. Make the extraordinary capability change the silhouette, construction and environmental relationships before adding surface detail.

## Build a varied set

Track the domains the user actually requests. If a brief asks for worlds, ships, monoliths, planetary structures, propulsion and weapons, represent all of them across the set rather than drifting entirely into ecological parables. Combine roles when the requested count is small. A singular brief needs only its requested subject.

Vary the premise, purpose, mechanism, silhouette, construction, environment, scale, viewpoint, light and palette. Keep the visual ambition and finish coherent when requested. An open ten-scene set can explore directions such as these; they are starting questions, not mandatory slots:

| Direction | Original concept opportunity | Visible construction to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep-space vessel | A migration ship carries a protected mountain range through a starless region | Long asymmetric hull, landscape retained in a deep open cradle, cold transport spines |
| Propulsion | A vessel temporarily moves its destination around a stationary body | Off-axis drive architecture, displaced background geometry, restrained exhaust or none |
| Alien monolith | An observatory accumulates geological time as material layers | Severe silhouette, exposed stratigraphy, tiny survey instruments, immense shadow |
| Planetary engineering | A civilization edits its planet's seasons with movable high-latitude structures | Curved limb, continent-scale shadows, distinct cloud systems and physical spans |
| Stellar engineering | Independent dark shutters shape a sun's illumination for distant ecologies | Sparse vast planes, stellar occlusion, molten edge light, great empty distances |
| Living architecture | A grown shipyard opens around a migrating biological vessel | Thick membranes, structural folds, tissue junctions, immense sheltered void |
| Fictional weapon | A planetary defense instrument turns a bounded volume temporarily unreachable | Severe aperture or distributed architecture, altered silhouette relationships, controlled activation |
| Ancient megastructure | A civilization inhabits only a seam in an unknown predecessor's great work | Almost uninterrupted mass, concentrated habitation, uncertain total form |
| Atmospheric civilization | Immense buoyant cities exchange weather within a gas giant | Membranes, suspended volumes, storm-depth layers, tiny transit craft |
| Cosmic encounter | A fleet meets a natural phenomenon larger than its known constructions | Distinct fleet silhouettes, environmental distortion, depth and incomplete revelation |

Avoid reusing one C-shaped drive, ring, wedge, portal, center-framed monument or orange/teal palette for every option. A familiar subject category can recur if its premise, form and spatial composition actually change.

## Make scale visible

For default monumental scenes, specify at least two compatible scale relationships, preferably at different levels. Use a single powerful relationship when the composition calls for greater simplicity. The goal is readable scale, not a quota of decorative cues.

- **Architectural:** a small service craft beneath a vast underside; repeating bays vanish into depth; one opening dwarfs inhabited terraces.
- **Landscape:** a structure interrupts an entire ridge or cloud front; near detail gives way to distant atmosphere; shadows cross geological features.
- **Planetary:** limb curvature, continent-scale weather, a moon behind an opening, a span crossing the visible hemisphere.
- **Stellar:** partial stellar occlusion, nested orbital distances, extreme sparse masses, local illumination dominated by the star.
- **Close system study:** a propulsion throat or weapon housing can fill the frame; cut edges, subsidiary components and receding repetition imply the greater structure beyond it.

Avoid shallow tabletop perspective, evenly sized decorative objects, domestic paving and cute rounded forms as the default visual translation. A tiny astronaut added to a small-object composition does not establish epic scale by itself. Do not use human railings on astronomical surfaces unless a particular accessible region is actually shown.

## Check before and after generation

**Before rendering:** remove titles and lore. Can the visual description still communicate an unfamiliar sci-fi world, convincing scale, coherent construction and atmosphere? If not, redesign its physical organization. Then audit diversity across the surviving candidates. Theme fit is not a score that novelty can compensate for.

**After rendering:** compare the actual image to the brief and selected references. Record whether the theme, subject, scale, spatial relationships and material hierarchy are visible. Count and topology claims require direct inspection; a prompt cannot verify itself. If the image collapses into a toy, a plaza, a near-future engineering plate or an abstract diagram, revise the principal forms, viewpoint and depth rather than adding microdetail to the failed composition. A technical explanation can be accurate while the artwork still fails this direction.

Preserve successful features when making a focused edit. If the concept itself is wrong for the brief and no user invariants require keeping it, generate a replacement from the corrected concept. Use the bounded iteration budget and state any remaining failure honestly. Retain the broader original-world ambition after the correction.
