Use these principles to connect a company logo, its artwork and its applications. Treat numerical values as starting proposals for the relevant construction family. They are neither measurements extracted from references nor universal requirements, approved brand standards or demonstrated production performance.

## Working idea

Make intelligence visible through a deliberate transformation: something changes position, joins, separates, makes room, repeats or preserves a recognisable relationship. Choose **one governing operation and one meaningful tension** for each direction.

Translate future engineering into observable geometry. A boundary may remain open; a path may resume across an interruption; a nested form may reverse solid and void. Speculative mastery of physics, mathematics, biology or materials supplies premises, not factual claims about what the image demonstrates.

Keep the result credible for a company landing page. Give the institution a memorable signature and restrained typography. Move into illustrative devices, human-machine bodies, habitats or cinematic environments only when the user changes the output focus.

Ordered paths are a useful branch: three or four routes undergo one precise transformation. They are not the definition of every logo. Also explore solid masses, reciprocal elements, open boundaries, nested forms and restrained monograms. Use the construction catalog to maintain that breadth.

## Geometry and negative space

Establish the silhouette first. Use a small vocabulary of masses, curves and terminals; let one exceptional relationship carry the character.

Draw the black spaces as deliberately as the white parts. Identify the entrance, route and end of an open channel, or the location and surrounding sides of an enclosed void. Preserve the principal negative-space event when simplifying or reducing a mark.

Use controlled asymmetry to make a familiar primitive less predictable: offset one opening, stagger one merger, shorten one shoulder or change one alignment. Keep the remaining construction consistent enough that this exception feels authored.

Separate continuity from physical connection. Aligned parts can suggest a route without touching. A filled bridge can join two regions. State which relationship is intended and inspect the rendered result rather than relying on the name of the concept.

## Construction proposals

For a substantial path-based mark, let **t** denote nominal path thickness and **H** emblem height:

| Element | Starting proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Paths | Three or four for a compact route-based symbol; other families may need one mass, two parts or a different sparse structure. |
| Main gaps | Begin around 0.65–0.85t, then judge optical balance and coupling. |
| Curves | Reuse a limited radius vocabulary and smooth tangent transitions where continuity is intended. |
| Terminals | Use a consistent flat or deliberately shaped treatment; avoid accidental needles. |
| Clear space | Begin at 0.5H around an isolated lockup. |
| Emblem-to-wordmark spacing | Begin around 0.4–0.6H, then tune for the actual silhouette and letterforms. |

Use these values to start a comparison, not to reject a stronger construction. Equal numerical gaps can look unequal. Widening a passage can improve separation while weakening the relationship between parts. Compare the relevant tradeoff.

Keep the primary mark flat and monochrome. Material studies may subsequently explore obsidian, ceramic or titanium depth, but preserve the same parts and voids. A compelling render does not repair a weak silhouette.

## Typography and colour

Render the resolved exact brand text. The default is lowercase **bene systems** on one baseline, using open letterforms, natural spacing and restrained weight. The installed skill identifier is not the wordmark.

Keep typography subordinate to the emblem. Subtle coordination of b, e and n proportions may support a custom wordmark; avoid turning every letter into an unrelated symbol. When testing geometry, hold the wordmark and presentation reasonably constant.

| Role | Proposed token | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Obsidian | `#08090A` | Primary background and large quiet areas. |
| Graphite | `#171A1D` | Secondary surfaces and subdued boundaries. |
| Off-white | `#F2F1EB` | Primary emblem and important type. |
| Secondary grey | `#ABB0B5` | Supporting text, checked in its actual context. |
| Cold accent | `#BBC8F2` | An optional active path, boundary or state in supporting artwork. |

Keep logo comparison cards monochrome by default. Use accent colour to identify something meaningful in the larger system, sparingly and without a glow treatment. Evaluate text contrast separately from intentionally subtle decorative linework.

## From emblem to environment

Use two levels of detail when extending a path family: a bold three- or four-path emblem, and approximately **24–48** finer paths in larger artwork. Carry forward the same opening, displacement, merger or interruption. Other construction families can instead expand their own boundary, nesting or adjacency rule.

Do not assume that every presentation needs a field. A plain dark card makes different logos easier to compare. Add a field, a header application or an enlarged detail only when it answers the requested design question.

For a landing page, reserve a calm region for the headline, supporting text and actions. Place the principal opening or bend in relation to a content edge. Allow the geometry to extend beyond the frame while preserving enough of its rule to remain coherent. Avoid high-contrast lines through text or controls.

Use the identity's proportions and negative space to influence alignment and cropping. Once a primary emblem is chosen, keep that company signature stable; related operations can supply supporting artwork without making every section appear to belong to a different company.

## Motion

Animate one parameter at a time: offset, curvature, opening width, merger position or registration across a seam. Name what stays recognisable and show settled states.

For ambient landing-page motion, a **10–14 second** cycle with a long settled interval is a starting prototype value. Judge it alongside real content. Keep the navigation logo stable and supply a static alternative for reduced-motion preferences.

Let movement reveal the rule. Avoid arbitrary glitching, particle traffic or perpetual rotation added merely to signal technology. A static sequence is a motion concept; do not describe it as a finished animation.

## Refinement and production

Preserve the chosen mark's defining relationship while testing a specific weakness. Review actual geometry, wordmark spelling and main/miniature correspondence after each render or correction.

When production assets are requested, redraw and inspect the vector at actual application sizes, including useful 24- and 32-pixel comparisons. Adjust optical gaps and verify positive/reversed versions. A miniature on a generated board does not establish those results.

Keep assistant recommendations separate from user selections and final identity approval. General enthusiasm supports continuing the exploration; it does not designate an approved master or prove a preference for every visible feature.
