# Aesthetic system

## Brand premise

Create original identity work for **bene systems**, an institution imagined in the year **3000** that engineers intelligence, bodies, materials, habitats and physical systems for humans and machines. Suggest abundant capability through calm structural decisions, precision and quiet institutional authority.

Use the latest explicitly supplied brand text when it differs from this default. Keep the company's identity credible on a real landing page. Express the distant future through relationships between forms; reserve literal devices, bodies and environments for those requested output modes.

Use speculative physics, mathematics and engineered biology as creative premises. A rendered shape is not evidence of a scientific discovery.

## Active visual grammar

- Keep the atmosphere overwhelmingly dark: obsidian, near-black, graphite, deep space and controlled ivory or cold titanium contrast.
- Establish core emblems as flat monochrome forms. Let silhouette and negative space carry their identity.
- Use clean contours, substantial sections, broad intentional terminals and robust joins. Strong lines may be straight or curved.
- Concentrate each direction in one governing geometric rule and one meaningful tension: connection, separation, nesting, interruption, reciprocity, scale or a shared boundary.
- Use ancient monumental proportion, geometric meanders, protein packing, cellular boundaries, bone structures, cybernetic articulation and algorithmic organisation as abstract construction sources.
- Explore symmetry as a rule, including reflection or rotation; allow deliberate asymmetry. Do not force a B monogram.
- Keep generous darkness and subordinate readable typography. Spell the default lowercase wordmark exactly **bene systems**, or use the user's resolved exact brand text.

For a core-logo presentation, show one large emblem, one restrained wordmark and one small flat duplicate with the same principal geometry. Add another purposeful detail only when requested. Keep layout, approximate visual weight and type treatment comparable when geometry is the experiment.

Use [the design language](design-language.md) for construction proposals, typography, colour and derived fields. Use [the construction catalog](construction-catalog.md) to move among path, boundary, mass, reciprocal, nested and typographic families. Preserve a shared discipline without forcing a shared silhouette.

## Human reading and geometric intrigue

Aim for a clear whole, then a legible relationship between parts, then a detail worth discovering. Use a small vocabulary of masses and contour decisions. Assess whether entrances, internal passages, corner transitions and section weights support that hierarchy.

Treat greater regularity, wider gaps and fewer components as hypotheses to compare. More clearance can weaken coupling; simplification can remove character. Preserve the selected mark's distinctive relationship while testing a particular weakness.

Distinguish visual judgment from measured performance. A miniature supports a visual comparison; it does not establish recognition speed, fabrication tolerance or exact vector identity.

## Evolving preferences

Read the current user's instructions and project state before choosing a branch. Explicit selections take precedence over assistant shortlists. General approval or “continue” does not select every previous image. Do not import another user's choices or assume an unavailable reference's appearance.

For refinement, preserve the identified parent and change only the requested properties. For radical exploration, keep the brand constants while varying how the identity is constructed.

## Avoid predictable drift

Avoid stock rockets, planets, atoms, cubes, toruses, AI sparkles, helmet-like heads, literal historical symbols, decorative equations, fake microtext, arbitrary alien lettering, circuit or HUD wallpaper, repeated stacked plates, rainbow chrome and excessive glow. Avoid accidental needles, fragile joins and gaps that disappear.

Material studies may use restrained obsidian, ceramic or titanium depth and narrow edge light. Assess their geometry against the flat mark. Allow richer detail for explicitly requested objects or environments while retaining one coherent structural rule.
