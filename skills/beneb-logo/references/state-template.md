## Useful learning

Record only durable observations that affect the next decision. Distinguish a speculative premise, the visible target, the observed result and any measured evidence. Retain superseded observations as historical when their comparison remains useful.

Do not turn a subjective clarity judgment into a claim about tested human recognition. Do not promote a current working ratio, corner radius or silhouette into a permanent brand requirement without user evidence.
