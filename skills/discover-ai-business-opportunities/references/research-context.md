## Core thesis

When intelligence and software become cheap, value creation and profit capture separate. Look for a paid job that becomes cheaper to deliver with AI while the business builds a reason to keep earning the purchase: efficient capacity, repeat buyer access, accepted measurements, operating relationships, lawful scarce inputs or real contractual economics. None is protected merely because it is physical, regulated or called a network.

Demand volume, buyer spending and supplier margin are different measures. More consumption can coexist with falling price. Pain does not establish a solvent payer. A feature being difficult to build today does not establish a durable moat. A customer's excellent internal AI is a competitor too.

## Historical seed hypotheses

These hypotheses are unvalidated. Their fit depends on the current founder's actual capabilities, access, capital and constraints. Do not treat a seed as fresh evidence, a user preference, a proven business, or a message already sent.

1. **Narrow infrastructure operations:** complete one defined recurring change/recovery process for private or on-premises infrastructure customers. Unknowns: reachable buyer, budget, delegated authority, exception cost and competition from MSPs and platforms.
2. **SaaS evidence upkeep:** own follow-through in an existing compliance platform for small SaaS firms. Source B2B-02 reports an Australian Vanta user seeking one or two hours of weekly help. This is a buyer report and help request, not a verified service purchase or consulting budget. Keep independent audit responsibility separate.
3. **MSP renewal reconciliation:** prevent missed renewal decisions and complete the operating cycle. Source B2B-03 reports financial exposure. A loss is not incremental willingness to pay; scripts, distributors and existing platforms are alternatives.
4. **Equipment commissioning and correction:** work through a maintenance provider to close callbacks, incomplete handovers or unresolved performance issues. Custom hardware is justified only by a repeated measurement or delivery gap. Verify who pays for failure, repeat provider orders, site access, licensing, liability and full costs. Public source examples include physical_08 and physical_17.
5. **Reliable local household/care support:** agreed tasks, continuity and cover for a clearly funded segment. Sources consumer_01 through consumer_04 show reported use or purchases, not proof of a new coordination fee. Labour, travel, staffing and reduced customer management work remain unproven. Care need alone does not establish affordability.
6. **Recurring offline activities:** classes, clubs and hosted experiences with continuing value after introductions. Local density, attendance, retention and discretionary income govern the business. Paid participation and churn anecdotes are research leads, not representative retention data.
7. **Institution-funded incident resolution:** actual assistance after identity or service problems, with an institution rather than a distressed individual as possible payer. This remains a weaker hypothesis; free services and incumbent resolution channels are material alternatives.

Generic AI advisors, dashboards, narrow utilities, setup agencies and creative tools are not ruled out. They need a specific buyer, distribution or completed workflow that beats low-cost substitutes. Transition cash-flow businesses can be rational without being permanently defensible.

## Fit with other workflows

When implementation is later authorized, use the appropriate engineering, hardware, design or operational skills rather than turning this research skill into a delivery agent.
